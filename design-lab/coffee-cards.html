The specimen card · with printed-label preview

The image column is replaced with a designed label panel — a typographic sticker preview that could print on N2G's coffee bags. Each panel uses N2G's brand palette graded by roast level: cream paper for X.O. (lightest), forest green for Special (mid), coffee-dark for Classic (darkest). The proprietary roast band, flavor list, and spec strip all stay on the card body to the right.

The label panel doubles as two things at once: the card's visual hero (since the bag photographs aren't earning their slot) and a glimpse of what future printed packaging could look like. Same brand voice — EB Garamond serif name, italic Cormorant flavor tagline, Karla micro-meta — just composed for a bag instead of for a screen.

Considered alternatives

Three directions explored before landing on the Specimen Card. Each is mocked at thumbnail size below — we don't recommend any of them, but the reasoning is preserved here for future reference.

Cinnamon → Full City Roast as color
A · Roast-as-color

Each panel uses the literal roast color of the SKU's beans — X.O. pale cream, Classic warm tan, Special espresso-dark. Educational, semantically clean. Why declined: the audience is wholesale/HORECA who already know roast levels by name. Loses the brand-palette spread. All three panels become coffee-bean-tone family — visually flatter on the shelf. We'd also lose the forest-green Special panel which is iconic.

B · Subtext-style poster

Bold display type, color blocks per SKU, the bag treated as a typographic poster (à la Subtext, Onyx, Rook). Visually striking. Why declined: N2G is multi-product (coffee, tea, produce, events). A loud Subtext idiom on coffee would either make it an island that clashes with the editorial tea grid + about page, or force a redesign of every other section to match. The visual punch isn't worth the cohesion cost.

Filter · AA-A Drip Select X.O. Brown sugar, orange blossom, lychee.
C · Tea-card parity

Smallest possible move — rebuild coffee cards using the existing tea-grid pattern (bordered card, photo on top, eyebrow + serif name + italic note). Gets you 80% of the way for very little code. Why declined: photos were the original problem (bag photos do the wrong job; no alternative photo type carries the SKU's identity). Also leaves the proprietary roast band + the brief's lot description with nowhere to live — the most N2G-specific information would stay invisible.

Tokens & swap-points

For the eventual integration into index.html — confirming the shippable change is contained.

Reused N2G tokens (no changes)

--cream --sand --text --green --green-dark --terra --gold
  • Type scale --t-eyebrow · --t-meta · --t-h2 · --t-h2-lg
  • Spacing scale --s1…--s10 · line-heights --lh-*
  • Fonts — EB Garamond (display) · Cormorant Garamond (italic) · Karla (UI)
  • Label-panel palette spread across brand colors: X.O. uses --cream + --green-dark; Special uses --green-dark + --cream; Classic uses cream warm + --terra accent. Brand-as-color committed; no roast-darkness gradient.
  • Gold (--gold) is the unifying hairline rule on X.O. + Special panels and the leading rule on the body+finish line.
  • Existing classes preserved — .sec-label · .accent · the whole tea grid · .flav chips (still used elsewhere)

New (prototype-only — promote on adoption)

--roast-cinnamon --roast-light --roast-city --roast-medium --roast-full-city
  • .label-panel & .label-panel-c — full + compact designed sticker previews (3:4 + 4:3)
  • .lp-light / .lp-mid / .lp-warm — three brand-graded themes (X.O. cream-on-green, Special forest, Classic cream-on-terra)
  • .lp-mark-b — ring-mark + serif italic "N2G" logo (matches Treatment B in logo-lab.html)
  • .lp-name--long — font-size override for long lot names ("ExtraOrdinary"); .lp-name-sub / .spec-name-sub / .lpc-name-sub — subtitle treatments for the X.O. unfolded form
  • .lp-flavors + .lp-cup — split tasting block: flavor profile (primary) + body+finish (supporting, with leading gold/terra rule)
  • .spec-card — editorial card container; .spec-description — gold-rule italic pull-quote of the brief's lot description
  • .spec-roast-track & .spec-roast-scale — the proprietary roast band
  • .today-frame — dashed-border reference frame around the "current site" cards
  • JetBrains Mono — added for micro-meta only (9–11px). Optional; could substitute Karla 500 if a third font isn't desired.
